Benchmark classifier: notebook/03-classifier_benchmark.py

File nay dung de so sanh cac classifier co dien voi feed-forward neural network tren bai toan binary one-vs-rest cho tung ho transcription factor (TF).

Mac dinh script chay theo split 80/20 da co san trong repo, tuong ung protocol cua paper:

train: data/mix_data/trainset/<family>.csv
test:  data/mix_data/testset/<family>.csv

Moi file <family>.csv co:

  • sequence: protein sequence.
  • class: nhan binary, 1 la family dang xet, 0 la cac family con lai.

Tien xu ly

Voi moi family va moi gia tri k, script lam:

  1. Doc train/test theo paper_split.
  2. Tinh k-mer frequency tu protein sequence.
  3. Fit DictVectorizer tren train, transform test.
  4. Chon dac trung bang ANOVA SelectKBest(f_classif).
  5. Fit baseline model tren train va danh gia tren test.

Rieng fnn_pretrained khong fit tren train. Model nay load weights co san va chi predict tren test.

Mac dinh:

  • --k 3
  • --max-rows 0: dung full data.
  • --random-state 42
  • --repeats 1
  • --k345-features 1000

Seed anh huong toi sampling va cac model co random_state.

Model va hyperparameter

Ten trong script Model Hyperparameter dat thu cong
knn KNeighborsClassifier n_neighbors=5
xgb XGBClassifier n_estimators=200, max_depth=6, learning_rate=0.05, subsample=0.9, colsample_bytree=0.9, eval_metric="logloss", random_state, n_jobs
random_forest RandomForestClassifier n_estimators=200, random_state, n_jobs
adaboost AdaBoostClassifier n_estimators=200, random_state
gaussian_nb GaussianNB default
svc_linear SVC kernel="linear", random_state
svc_rbf SVC kernel="rbf", random_state
fnn_pretrained Keras feed-forward neural network load tu models/Binary-Classifier/<family>/FEEDFORWARD_k{k}.keras

Nhung tham so khac dung default cua thu vien tuong ung.

FNN pretrained

fnn_pretrained khong train lai. No load model .keras da co san va dung features_mask trong meta.json de lay dung 1000 features tu vector k-mer.

Nguon file:

models/Binary-Classifier/<family>/FEEDFORWARD_k3.keras
models/Binary-Classifier/<family>/meta.json

Prediction threshold:

0.5

Can ghi ro day la pretrained FNN, khong phai FNN duoc train lai cung baseline.

Co optimize hyperparameter khong?

Khong.

Script khong dung:

  • grid search
  • random search
  • Bayesian optimization
  • cross-validation de chon hyperparameter
  • tuning rieng cho tung family

Vi vay ket qua la benchmark voi mot bo hyperparameter co dinh, khong phai so sanh sau khi toi uu tung model.

Co the doi output:

--output path/to/detail.csv
--summary-output path/to/summary.csv
--comparison-output path/to/comparison.csv

Luu y khi dien giai ket qua

  • Neu so sanh baseline voi fnn_pretrained, day la so sanh baseline train lai voi FNN pretrained co san trong repo.
  • fnn_pretrained van danh gia tren cung test split, nen co gia tri tham khao tot.
  • Tuy nhien, no khong hoan toan cong bang nhu train lai FNN tu dau voi cung seed va cung dieu kien.

Nhan xet chinh:

  • fnn_pretrained vuot ro svc_linear va knn. F1 trung binh cao hon svc_linear khoang 0.24 va cao hon knn khoang 0.03.
  • So voi nhom model manh hon nhu xgb, random_forest, adaboost, va svc_rbf, fnn_pretrained rat canh tranh. Trung binh F1 chi thap hon khoang 0.0007 den 0.0055.
  • fnn_pretrained thang tren nhieu family hon so voi tung baseline manh: thang xgb o 43/58 family, thang random_forest o 44/58 family, thang adaboost o 43/58 family, va thang svc_rbf o 52/58 family.
  • Mot vai family lam giam F1 trung binh cua fnn_pretrained, dac biet DBB va EIL. Vi cac outlier nay, xgb, random_forest, va adaboost co F1 trung binh cao hon mot chut.
  • Ve toc do, fnn_pretrained khong train lai, nen cot train_time_sec cua model nay nen hieu la thoi gian load model. Thoi gian load/predict cua no rat nho so voi cac model phai train lai, dac biet la svc_linear.

Ket luan:

fnn_pretrained khong dat F1 trung binh cao nhat trong file ket qua nay; xgb dung dau theo F1 trung binh. Tuy vay, fnn_pretrained van dat hieu nang rat cao, on dinh tren phan lon family, vuot xa knn va svc_linear, va canh tranh sat voi cac ensemble models. Ket qua nay ung ho viec dung FNN pretrained cua MegaPlantTF nhu mot mo hinh manh va thuc dung, nhung neu ket luan theo benchmark cong bang nghiem ngat thi can ghi ro rang FNN nay la pretrained, khong duoc train lai trong cung dieu kien voi cac baseline.
